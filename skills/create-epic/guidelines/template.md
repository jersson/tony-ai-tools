# Epic Template

```markdown
# Epic: <title>

## Summary
<one paragraph: what this epic is about and why it matters>

## Problem / Opportunity
<the pain or opportunity, and why now>

## Goal / Outcome
<what changes when this succeeds — measurable or verifiable>

## Target Users
- <persona/role>: <their need>

## Scope
### In Scope
- <capability / outcome area>
### Out of Scope
- <explicitly excluded — prevents scope creep>

## Success Metrics
- <metric or verifiable signal>

## Assumptions & Constraints
- <assumption or constraint>

## Dependencies & Risks
- <dependency or risk>

## Candidate User Stories
- <hint of a story to expand later with create-user-story>
```

## Naming

- File name: kebab-case of the epic title (e.g., `referral-program.md`)
- Location: `<project_root>/docs/epics/`
