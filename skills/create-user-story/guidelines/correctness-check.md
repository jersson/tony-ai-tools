# Content Correctness Criteria

Analyze each drafted story for logical consistency, domain accuracy, and internal contradictions:

- Do the acceptance criteria conflict with each other?
- Is the role appropriate for the action described?
- Does the benefit logically follow from the action?
- Are there implicit assumptions that should be made explicit?
- Do the acceptance criteria actually verify the narrative (not a different feature)?
- Is any criterion untestable as written (subjective words like "fast", "nice", "easy")? Rewrite it with a concrete threshold or observable behavior.
