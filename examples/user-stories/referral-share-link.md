# Referral Share Link

As a registered customer,
I want to share a personal referral link,
so that I can earn store credit when friends make their first purchase.

## Acceptance Criteria
1. The account page shows a "Refer a friend" section with a unique link per customer
2. Clicking "Copy" places the full referral link on the clipboard and shows a confirmation
3. A signup completed through the link is attributed to the sharing customer within 5 minutes
4. Links remain valid for 12 months from creation
