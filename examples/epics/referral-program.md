# Epic: Referral Program

## Summary
A referral program that rewards existing customers for inviting friends, turning the customer base into a growth channel with traceable, attributable signups.

## Problem / Opportunity
Paid acquisition cost per signup has grown 40% year over year while word-of-mouth signups convert at twice the average rate. There is currently no mechanism to encourage or attribute word-of-mouth.

## Goal / Outcome
10% of new signups within two quarters of launch come through referral links.

## Target Users
- Existing customer: wants a reward for recommending a product they already like
- Invited friend: wants a frictionless first experience with an immediate incentive

## Scope
### In Scope
- Unique referral link generation per customer
- Reward granting for both sides after the friend's first purchase
- Referral dashboard showing sent invites and earned rewards

### Out of Scope
- Cash payouts (rewards are store credit only)
- Multi-level / pyramid referral structures
- Fraud detection beyond basic self-referral blocking

## Success Metrics
- Referral share of new signups ≥ 10% by end of Q4
- ≥ 25% of active customers generate at least one link

## Assumptions & Constraints
- Store credit infrastructure already exists
- Rewards budget is capped at 5% of monthly revenue

## Dependencies & Risks
- Depends on transactional email service for invite delivery
- Risk: reward abuse via disposable emails — mitigated by first-purchase gating

## Candidate User Stories
- Customer shares a personal referral link
- Friend signs up through a referral link and gets a first-purchase discount
- Customer sees referral status and earned credit in their dashboard
